# Movement stories

The museum guide shows one of these texts after it recognises the style of a painting.

**How to edit**
- Replace every `TODO`. Keep the `##` headings and the bold labels exactly as they are: the guide reads them, and the headings are the class names used by the model.
- Start each field on a new line with its bold label. A field can run over several lines.
- Story: at most 120 words. What to look for: at most 60 words. Message: at most 40 words. Write for a visitor reading on a phone in front of the painting.
- Every fact must be checkable: list your sources at the end of the file.
- `pytest tests/test_stories.py` checks the format and the word limits.
